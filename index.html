Defend Your Farm

A NASA-powered climate survival game. Real weather from NASA satellites and models hits your field one day at a time. Drought, heat, wind, storms, pests and frost are coming, and you protect your crops with the same practices real farmers use.

Real dataEvery day of weather is a real day from NASA POWER for the farm location you choose.
Real agronomyEach plot tracks soil water, topsoil, pests and crop health, and responds to irrigation, mulch, cover crops, windbreaks, drainage and hoop covers.
Learn by doingSix stages teach one practice each, then you plan a full-year crop rotation within your own budget and water.
GUIDE

Howdy. I'm Hank. My crops keep failing and the sky keeps changing. NASA's satellites can see what's coming — help me turn their data into a farm that survives.
